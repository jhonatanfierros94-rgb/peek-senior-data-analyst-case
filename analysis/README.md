# Part 2 — Product & Business Analysis

## Looker Studio Dashboard

The dashboard presents the three required analytical views:

1. Monthly business health — revenue, gross profit, and orders
2. New vs. returning revenue mix alongside 90-day churn
3. Recent 12 mature months vs. the preceding 12 months

**Dashboard:** [Open Looker Studio Dashboard]([https://datastudio.google.com/s/hyOixQoW8MY](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/53d6cdce-cd25-44c7-a218-c10a6c7c38cb))

### Analysis window

- Commerce metrics: February 2019 – September 2026
- Latest mature 90-day churn month: June 2026
- Analysis as-of date: 2026-10-21

Detailed methodology, recommendations, limitations, and AI workflow are documented in the repository root `README.md`.
